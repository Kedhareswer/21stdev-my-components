# chladni-plate: critic rounds

Each round is a fresh critic subagent that saw only forge/BRIEF.md, the
screenshots (SwiftShader) and the source.

## Idea gate (2026-10-13)

Five candidates went to a fresh critic: chladni-plate, ebru-marbling-tray,
pinscreen-wordmark, shoreline-swash, flip-dot-sign. Picked: **chladni-plate**.
The rest were rejected as a repeat of ink pieces (marbling), a desk toy
(pinscreen), a remake of raked-sand-garden (shoreline) and a retro gimmick
(flip-dot). Risks flagged: iron-filings déjà vu (the brass must carry it),
neon science-demo drift, a discoverable bow, crisp lines without fizz, a
finished first frame, SwiftShader cost.

## Round 1

| | Score |

Verdict: **REDO** (average 6.75, craft below 7).

Changes asked for, most important first:

1. Sand reads as chalk: even-width speckled ribbons, dark speckle edges. Settle
   by amplitude so ridges are fat at crossings and thin on steep slopes,
   taller heaps, a little loose dust.
2. The bow lies flat over the brass, covers the headline in the aside layout,
   and the auto stroke runs off-frame. A Chladni bow is drawn upright against
   the edge: render it foreshortened, with a cast shadow that changes through
   the stroke.
3. Plate edge thickness, a cool environment term, a subtler spun finish than
   the wedge streak, a nickel clamp that reads as metal.
4. Sand in flight reads as smoke puffs: draw discrete specks.
5. Light theme: the lamp pool sits behind the copy (low contrast); the day
   bench needs real soapstone character.
6. Phone: a dead band between plate and copy; moiré in the lathe lines.
7. The plough barely shows.
8. Pill 44 px on coarse pointers, live reduced-motion, chunk the
   reduced-motion settle, README wording on the plate model.
