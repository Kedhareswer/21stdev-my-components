# Craft Beer Product Template

A full product landing page for one beer from one brewery. The demo brand is
**Toofan Brewing Co.**, an invented craft brewery in Pune, and its Monsoon
Season IPA.

The bottle carries the page. It rides the scroll on a sticky layer and the
sections move past it:

1. **Hero.** A two-line headline with the bottle tilted across it. Scrolling
   stands the bottle up, and the brewery seal turns.
2. **Details.** A handwritten kicker, the beer's name, an intro, a buy button
   and a box of numbers (ABV, IBU, OG).
3. **Mouthfeel.** One giant word (*Chatkara*, the smack of the lips after
   something good) slides behind the bottle. A video card sits on the left
   and the tasting notes on the right.
4. **Flavours.** Three ink splats in turmeric, tulsi green and jaggery brown
   pop in with the flavour notes, and a full mug tips in from the bottom left.
5. **Availability.** The pack line-up. The bottle shrinks and drops into its
   empty slot in the row, then scrolls away with it.
6. **Recipe pairing** and a **footer**.

The look is *monsoon night*: a rain-slate ground with fine slanted rain
drifting over it, an amber glow behind the bottle, Yatra One display type,
Mukta for text and Kalam handwriting.

## Requires

- `gsap` (with `ScrollTrigger`)
- Tailwind is not needed. All styles are in one scoped `<style>` block (`.tb-*`).

## Props

| Prop | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `bottle` | none | Upright, front-on bottle cut-out (transparent PNG/WebP/AVIF, about 0.27 : 1). Without it, no bottle is shown. |
| `glass` | none | A full glass, cut out. |
| `videoPoster`, `videoHref` | none, `#video` | The video card is a link; point it at your film. |
| `recipeImage` | none | 3 : 2 food photo. |
| `packs` | 5 packs | `{ label, detail?, src?, alt?, scale?, slot? }`. `scale` is the height relative to the tallest pack. The pack with `slot: true` stays empty, and the travelling bottle lands in it. |
| `stats` | ABV, IBU, OG | `{ label, sub, value, unit?, note, href? }`. `unit` is drawn smaller. |
| `flavours` | 3 notes | `{ title, body, icon: "citrus" \| "leaf" \| "steam", tone: "haldi" \| "tulsi" \| "gud" }`. The first three are used. |
| `headline`, `title`, `kicker`, `intro`, `feelWord`, `tasting`, `style`, `availability`, `recipeTitle`, ... | Toofan copy | All text is data. |
| `brand`, `brandSub`, `buyLabel`, `buyHref`, `sealText`, `footerLines`, `legal` | Toofan | Chrome and footer. |
| `tilt` | `24` | The hero tilt of the bottle, in degrees. |
| `height` | `"100svh"` | The viewport height the sticky stage is sized against. |
| `fontHref` | Google Fonts URL | Kalam, Mukta, Yatra One (all OFL). Pass `null` to load your own. |

## Behaviour

- On desktop with motion allowed, the bottle stands up over the first 75 % of
  a viewport of scroll, holds through the middle sections and lands in its
  slot. The landing is measured from layout offsets, so entrance tweens can't
  shift it.
- On phones and portrait tablets (900 px and narrower) and with `prefers-reduced-motion`, the bottle
  doesn't travel. It sits in the hero and in its slot. On phones it still
  stands up as the hero scrolls away.
- No ScrollTrigger pinning. Everything is in one `gsap.context` and
  `gsap.matchMedia`, reverted on unmount.

## Assets in the demo

Every photo in `demo.tsx` was generated by us with Canva `generate-image` on
2026-11-04 for this invented brand, then cut out and inlined as AVIF data
URIs. The bottles and tap were cut out locally; the mug and cans with
Adobe's background removal. No stock or third-party photos are used. Sources
and prompts: `media/craft-beer-product-template/ASSETS.md` in the authoring
repo.
